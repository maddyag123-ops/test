Featured Projects

Food, Water, and Communities

In 2019, my colleague and I created this ArcGIS StoryMap platform to address three key challenges in science communication: the tendency to portray communities as victims, the difficulty of grasping the global scope of climate impacts, and the lack of paid opportunities for student science writers.

As co-creator, writer, and lead editor of this platform sponsored by The Center for Communicating Science at Virginia Tech, I helped scientists share stories from around the globe about communities whose food and water resources have been impacted by climate change. By focusing on communities rather than just science, we created a new, empathetic approach to science communication.

Science Communication Training

Teaching researchers how to translate complex scientific findings into compelling narratives that resonate with diverse audiences. I've developed and led workshops including Three Minute Thesis (3MT) training for graduate students and ComSciCon workshops, helping scientists communicate their work effectively to policymakers, stakeholders, and the general public.

Through these training programs, I help researchers develop storytelling skills that make their work accessible, memorable, and impactful beyond academic circles.

Stakeholder Engagement & Facilitation

Facilitating workshops and co-design exercises as part of my research methodology to learn from communities and integrate their knowledge into scientific research. This work goes beyond teaching—it's about creating spaces where stakeholders can share their expertise, values, and lived experiences that inform better research outcomes.

Through facilitation techniques, I help bridge the gap between scientific inquiry and community wisdom, ensuring research addresses real-world needs and reflects diverse perspectives.
