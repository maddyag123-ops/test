Featured Project

Integrating Values into Water Management

My doctoral research develops innovative methods to integrate stakeholder values into water management decision-making tools for the Goulburn-Broken River Catchment in Australia. By bridging the gap between what communities care about and how managers make decisions, this work creates pathways for more inclusive, effective water governance.
